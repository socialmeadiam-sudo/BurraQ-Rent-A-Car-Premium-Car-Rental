Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our car rental services.

What types of cars are available for rent?

We offer a wide range of vehicles including Toyota Corolla, Honda Civic, Toyota Hiace, Suzuki Alto, Toyota Fortuner, Suzuki Every, and more. Contact us on WhatsApp for the latest availability.

Do you provide cars with drivers?

Yes! We provide both options — rent a car with an experienced driver or opt for self-drive if you prefer to drive yourself. Self-drive requires a valid driving license and documents.

How do I book a car with BurraQ Rent A Car?

Booking is easy! Just message us on WhatsApp at 0312 0017534 or call us. Tell us your requirements, date, and destination — we'll confirm your booking right away.

Do you provide airport pickup and drop-off?

Yes, we provide reliable airport transfer services at Karachi Airport. We monitor flight schedules to ensure on-time pickup and drop-off for our customers.

Are your cars available for outstation trips?

Absolutely! We offer cars for outstation trips to Hyderabad, Thatta, and other cities. Contact us for outstation packages and pricing.

Where is BurraQ Rent A Car located?

We are located at Sector 11 Orangi Town, Karachi, 75800, Pakistan. You can visit us or contact us via WhatsApp for bookings.
